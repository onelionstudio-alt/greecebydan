A few things to know

These helpers suggest options from the results returned by Viator; they don’t compare every experience in Greece, and a suggestion isn’t a personal inspection or a guarantee. I haven’t personally tried every experience shown here.

You’ll book on Viator, under the terms shown there. The local tour operator provides the experience. Greece by Dan doesn’t take payments, operate tours or manage bookings. For booking changes or refunds, use Viator’s booking support; for trip arrangements and requirements, contact the operator.

Prices, itineraries and availability can change. Boat trips also depend on weather and sea conditions. The helpers can’t confirm suitability for your individual needs. Nothing here changes your statutory rights.

For wine experiences, plan a safe way back and don’t drive after drinking.
