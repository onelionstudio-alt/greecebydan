Privacy Policy

This site is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible.

Hosting and technical data

Greece by Dan is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most web hosting services, infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs for security and operation of the service.

External and affiliate links

When you follow a link to another website, that provider’s privacy policy applies. Affiliate partners may use referral parameters or cookies to attribute a booking or purchase.

Analytics

Greece by Dan uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page views, popular stories and referring websites. This service does not use cookies or local storage to track visitors. See Cloudflare Web Analytics for details.

Travel helpers

The concierge helpers send your selected destination and preferences to a separate Cloudflare Worker. Destination, category, price and cancellation filters are used to search Viator’s API. Cloudflare processes technical request data, including IP addresses, for service operation and rate limiting. The helpers do not ask for your name, email or payment details and do not create a visitor profile. Search results are briefly cached; destination and category lists are cached for a week. When you click through to Viator, its privacy policy applies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page.
