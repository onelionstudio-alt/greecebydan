Privacy Policy

Greece by Dan is an independent travel website operated by Dan Greenberg in Israel. For privacy questions or requests, contact mygreece@greecebydan.com.

What happens when you visit

GitHub Pages hosts the website and Cloudflare provides technical services. These providers may process your IP address, browser and request information, timestamps and security logs to deliver pages, prevent abuse and operate their services. Turning off optional analytics does not stop this necessary technical processing.

Using the travel helpers

When you use the concierge, your selected destination and preferences are sent to our Cloudflare Worker. Destination, experience category, starting-price and cancellation/private-tour filters are used to search Viator. Other selected preferences help filter the returned options. We do not ask for your name, email address, payment details, precise location or health information in the helper, and we do not build a persistent visitor profile from your searches.

Search responses containing product information may be cached for up to 15 minutes; destination and category reference data may be cached for a week. These caches are not personal search histories. Cloudflare may process IP addresses for security and rate limiting. The site does not make bookings or collect payments.

Contacting us by email

If you email mygreece@greecebydan.com, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards your message to our Gmail inbox, provided by Google. We use your email address, message and any information you choose to send to respond to your enquiry and handle related requests. Only include information needed for your enquiry. You can read the site and use the travel helpers without emailing us.

Optional analytics are off

This version does not send our optional interaction events or load our manually embedded Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon. We do not use optional analytics cookies, local storage identifiers or fingerprinting. We also do not load Viator product photographs in this version.

If we introduce optional analytics, we will first update this notice with the provider, purpose, data fields, retention period and any relevant international transfers. Where consent is required, collection will start only after a separate, informed choice. Accepting will not be a condition of reading the site or using the helper; rejecting will be equally available. You will be able to withdraw consent as easily as giving it, without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Continuing to browse will not count as consent. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals will disable our optional interaction collector if it is later enabled.

Links, donations and booking partners

Viator, Click&Boat, Buy Me a Coffee, YouTube and other linked services have their own privacy policies. A link does not load their player or donation widget on this site. When you choose to visit an external service, it may receive referral information, set cookies or use similar technologies under its own rules. Affiliate links contain referral parameters that may attribute a qualifying booking to Greece by Dan. We do not receive your payment details through these links.

Purposes and legal grounds

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, necessary website delivery, security and responding to enquiries rely on our legitimate interests in providing and protecting the service, subject to your rights and the required balancing of interests. Processing required by law relies on the relevant legal obligation. Optional processing that requires consent will rely on your prior consent. We do not use the concierge to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Retention

We do not maintain a visitor account database or a store of optional interaction events in this version. Search caches expire as described above. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any related legal requirements or disputes. Infrastructure providers apply their own technical and security retention arrangements. You can contact us for information about the data relevant to your request.

Providers and international processing

Hosting, security, email and booking services may involve processing outside your country, including outside Israel or the European Economic Area. Where applicable, such transfers require an adequacy decision or other lawful safeguards. Contact us for information about relevant providers and transfer arrangements. External booking partners are responsible for processing carried out on their own services.

Provider notices: GitHub Privacy Statement, Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. These explain the providers’ own processing; they do not replace our responsibilities for personal data we handle.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent for consent-based processing. Contact the address above; we may need proportionate information to verify your request. You may also complain to the data protection authority competent for your situation. These rights are subject to applicable conditions; we do not promise deletion of information we must lawfully retain.

Essential administrative sign-in may use authentication cookies or browser storage. It is separate from optional visitor analytics. See our Cookie Policy and Affiliate Disclosure. Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this notice.
