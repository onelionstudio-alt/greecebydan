Sikinos

Sikinos is a small Cycladic island between Ios and Folegandros. It is not a place built around a long checklist. That is precisely the point.

Why Sikinos

Come for stone paths, Aegean views, quiet Chora evenings and a pace that makes “doing very little” feel like a plan.

Episkopi

One of the island’s most remarkable sites is Episkopi, a Roman-era monument later transformed into a Christian church. Its long history makes it far more intriguing than its remote setting first suggests.

Historical and access details can change. Check current local and official information before visiting.

Chora

The island’s main settlement is made for wandering rather than navigating: whitewashed lanes, small squares, blue details and views that arrive between buildings.

Beaches

Sikinos is about simple swims rather than beach-club spectacle. Conditions and transport are seasonal, so leave room for the island to decide your day.

Getting there

Sikinos has no airport. Visitors normally reach the island by ferry, often connecting through Athens or another Cycladic island. Always check current ferry schedules before making fixed plans.
